General Application Process

The general application process for international migration typically involves the following steps:

  1. Initial Consultation: Our team of experienced professionals will conduct a consultation to understand your migration goals and provide personalized guidance.
  2. Document Preparation: We will help you gather and prepare all necessary documents for your migration application.
  3. Application Submission: We will submit your migration application on your behalf and provide ongoing support and guidance throughout the process.
  4. Follow-up: We will provide regular updates and support to ensure your migration application is processed efficiently and successfully.
